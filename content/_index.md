<p class="hebrew">חוה (<em>hava</em>) — breathing, living</p>

The Human-Aligned Video AI Laboratory studies how AI that interprets video can align with human values. We are an interdisciplinary PhD programme of the University of Amsterdam's Data Science Centre (DSC).

<p class="lead">What defines human-aligned video-AI, how can it be made computable, and what determines its societal acceptance?</p>

We study cognitive, ethical and legal alignment, and develop video AI for research in criminology, medicine, dentistry and online campaigns.
