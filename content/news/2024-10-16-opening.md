On **16 October 2024**, UvA rector magnificus Peter-Paul Verbeek officially opened HAVA-Lab at the University Library Singel. The lab is an interdisciplinary PhD programme hosted by the UvA Data Science Centre.

The programme brings together seven PhD candidates and researchers from all seven UvA faculties.

Our research asks: *What defines human-aligned video-AI, how can it be made computable, and what determines its societal acceptance?*

HAVA-Lab was selected through the DSC's 2023 Interdisciplinary Lighthouse Project call. Its proposal was ranked first by the review committee.

Three PhD projects focus on cognitive, ethical and legal alignment. Four further projects study applications in criminology, medical skill assessment, dental diagnostic training and responsible marketing.
